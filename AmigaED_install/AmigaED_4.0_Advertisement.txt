AmigaED 4.0 – The Modern IDE for Classic Amiga Development
      interfaces Bebbo's amiga-gcc and its forks
================================================================

Are you developing for AmigaOS 1.3 or 3.x and tired of switching back and forth between the editor,
compiler shell, and emulator? AmigaED 4.0 brings
complete cross-development for the classic Amiga into a
single, modern interface—built with Qt6, it runs natively on Windows,
Linux, and macOS.

Two Ways to Reach Your Goal
---------------------------
Whether it's a quick ad-hoc test of a single file or a complete,
multi-file project with a GUI: AmigaED adapts to your workflow.
For projects, the IDE automatically handles all Makefile management
—for vbcc, m68k-amigaos-gcc/g++, SAS/C, and now also for
pure m68k assembler projects in the vasm or GNU-as dialect, including
correctly configured linker calls.

Everything Under One Roof
--------------------------------------
- Five toolchains configured simultaneously and selectable with a single click:
  vbcc, GCC/G++, SAS/C, vasm, and GNU as
- Seven project templates—ranging from “Empty C” to AmigaShell, AmigaOS 1.3/3.x,
  ReAction and MUI GUI skeletons to the new assembler project
- Custom syntax highlighters for C/C++ (including AmigaOS NDK data types),
  m68k assembler, AmigaGuide hypertext, and installer scripts
- Color-coded compiler output with “click-to-jump” directly to the
  erroneous line—for each of the five toolchains
- Integrated start/stop for the UAE emulator, directly from the toolbar
- Automatic program icons, automatic math library detection,
  and many other small automated features that save you manual work
	
Looks great, feels familiar
----------------------------------------
Choose between a light and dark theme—or dive right into
Amiga nostalgia with the lovingly
recreated Workbench 1.3 and Workbench 3.1 looks. The entire interface can be switched between
English and German at runtime.

Free. Open. For the community.

----------------------------------------
AmigaED 4.0 is open-source software. The complete source code is
freely available and can be used, further developed, and adapted to your own
needs — entirely in the spirit of the Amiga community from
which it originates.

Whether you’re a retro hobbyist or a longtime Amiga veteran who wants to dive back into Assembler
or C for the Kickstart 1.3 or 3.x world: AmigaED 4.0
makes it fun again.
